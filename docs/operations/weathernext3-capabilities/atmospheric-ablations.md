Task 5 atmospheric comparisons are complete on the eligible retained evidence. No candidate passes the frozen selection gate, and all production temperature, wind, PV-cooling and thermal models remain unchanged. The complete cohort-specific metrics, sample sizes, supported subgroup regressions, fallback-inclusive comparisons and descriptive date-block intervals are in [the results record](atmospheric-experiment.json).

The final experiment replayed 274 immutable native forecast originals and verified 1,021 original input files. It considered 6,354 near-term temperature pairs, 1,738 scalar wind-hour pairs and 1,256 comparable thermal pairs. All 189 temperature pairs using recorded forecast ingestion were excluded because their retained origin-observation capture was later than the original decision. This leaves 9,159 paired forecasts across 13 source/quality/grid/lead cohorts. Repeated forecasts of the same measurement share target weight; forecast-row counts are not independent observations.

The historical experiment explicitly assumes forecast readiness at initialization plus nine hours and earlier-target training readiness. Actual later provider/measurement capture times and actual fit-completion timestamps remain retained. Recorded-ingestion cases require their real source and origin inputs before the decision and real training-label availability before the fit cutoff. The historical results are retrospective development evidence, not operational as-issued scores. Persisted numeric temperature history remains separate from receipt versions and hardware epochs; it acquires no sensor-health or metrology qualification.

Each group is tested individually against a separately refitted, otherwise identical base on the same eligible rows. Temperature uses the original seven forecast/origin residual predictors. Wind compares one gain-only benchmark with one bounded affine-log1p scalar-speed family, and then individually adds atmospheric groups to that same log-wind base. Thermal uses the same six total-only empirical response predictors and comparable recorded passive-action trajectories from Task 4. These new matched fits are not a reissue of installed models, an identified building heat-transfer model, a causal ventilation experiment, or a PV electrical/cooling correction.

Temperature dew point does not meet the gain criterion in any of the three observation cohorts. Pressure changes MAE from 1.8310 to 1.8252°C in the original v1-receipt cohort and from 1.9866 to 2.0209°C in the persisted-numeric cohort. In the original v2-receipt cohort it improves 1.7631 to 1.6757°C, but the supported 23-hour subgroup regresses by more than the allowed five percent. The incumbent is retained in every cohort.

The supported near-term wind comparison contains 167 outer measured targets across ten local dates (648 original forecast pairs). Gain-only MAE is 0.45635 m/s versus 0.45527 m/s for the bounded log-wind challenger: about 0.24% improvement, below the required three percent, with supported lead-subgroup regressions as well. Dew point and pressure added to the same log-wind base do not improve its mean error. Longer-lead and recorded-ingestion cohorts lack sufficient earlier fitting/outer support under the frozen rules; their fallback and original raw comparisons remain visible.

At the one-hour thermal horizon, adding dew point or pressure worsens the empirical base MAE from 0.8541°F to 0.9270°F or 0.9195°F. At six hours, the average improves from 2.8863°F to 2.6140°F or 2.5291°F, but supported local-hour subgroups exceed the allowed regression limit. Neither addition is selected. The 12-hour assessment has only two outer local dates and remains insufficient. There are no eligible 24-hour comparable trajectories: Task 4 already records the 576 action/completeness/barrier exclusions. These outcomes describe measured air-temperature response conditional on the retained trajectories, not physical facade calibration or causal action benefit.

No complete compatible wind-direction or cloud-layer feature inputs exist in this evaluated population. Those software paths are implemented and tested with original native-payload fixtures, but their empirical comparisons remain `insufficient_evidence`. Missing values, calm vectors and native-hour gaps are not imputed or replaced with future observations. Optional collection remains disabled in production; no new cloud access or account/billing change was made for this experiment.

The protocol remains the pre-assessment [frozen atmospheric policy](atmospheric-ablation-protocol.json), linked to the [general evaluation protocol](evaluation-protocol.json). It uses rolling past targets, training-only preprocessing and inner regularization choice, date→target→forecast weights, a 28-day maximum training window, explicit support minima, a single fit at a time and the original 120-second/10,000-row limits. The first run spent its budget replaying originals; equivalent temperature verification through the unchanged serving-feature function removed duplicate normalization without changing either rules or outcomes. Final execution took 91.88 seconds, retained 317 fitted artifacts, and recorded zero attempted network connections or child launches. Probabilities, temporal-independence, physical calibration and action authority remain unqualified.
