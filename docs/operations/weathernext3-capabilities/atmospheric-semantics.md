Each field is replayed from an original prospective snapshot and retains its own run, native asset/grid hashes, first availability, units and original source digest. Temperature/dewpoint diagnostics require compatible station cell, run, asset and target time. Later-acquired optional fields cannot enter an earlier context; unavailable optional components do not replace scalar speed or erase core fields. No spatial interpolation is introduced.

Derived RH uses the National Weather Service's [over-water vapor-pressure approximation](https://www.weather.gov/media/epz/wxcalc/vaporPressure.pdf): `e(T) = 6.11 × 10^(7.5T/(237.3+T))` in hPa for Celsius input, followed by `100 e(Td)/e(T)`. It is explicitly a diagnostic from mean temperature and mean dewpoint, not ensemble-mean RH, a calibrated probability or a condensation-safety result. Substantially crossed mean temperatures withhold the derived diagnostic instead of silently clipping it. No building-surface temperature is inferred.

Wind-from direction uses the negative mean u/v vector: eastward u=1,v=0 is from west270°, southward u=0,v=−1 is from north0°. Calm, cancelling, missing or indeterminate components have unavailable direction. The magnitude of the mean vector and the provided mean scalar speed stay separate; neither is labeled as a gust. Optional high/medium/low cloud percentages remain separate from total cover and are not summed, since layers can overlap.

Pressure is labeled mean sea-level pressure in hPa. A diagnostic difference is available only when the observed convention is explicitly also mean sea level; station pressure and unknown conventions do not receive an implicit conversion. These functions do not change the temperature model, PV cooling wind, ventilation model, or any action authority.
