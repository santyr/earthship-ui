The reconstruction normalizes a constant nonnegative daylight DNI shape over complete retained five-minute geometry, so its horizontal integral equals FDIR. Diffuse energy is SSRD minus FDIR, distributed over the same daylight support. It conserves the raw hourly energies before applying the existing terrain mask to direct radiation. Diffuse sky view remains unmodeled. Missing direct data, substantially crossed energies, low-sun bounds and missing full source-hour geometry each produce a named total-only fallback. Missing geometry inside the requested prediction window withholds that projection. A partial remaining hour never receives the complete original hour's energy.
