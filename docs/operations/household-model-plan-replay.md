# Household model-to-plan replay

The pure `household.pipeline` boundary now rederives daily energy arithmetic, the prepared thermal trajectory, model-triggered candidate intents, hard constraints and the complete selected shadow plan. All decisions share one frozen snapshot. Numerical SoC must equal the snapshot's battery value; thermal measured rows and origin clocks must match exactly. The existing latent mass state remains separately labeled.

`FrozenEnergyInputs` retains finite bounded exact inputs for the existing daily equations. The production worker records its exact argument map in `household_energy_inputs` inside its existing prediction state; no new live writer or publication is added. Earlier rounded diagnostic records cannot recover these exact arguments and are not backfilled. Capacity, efficiency, resource/demand calibration and overnight sample qualification remain caller-owned: numerical inputs explicitly have qualification `not_assessed`.

Candidate generation uses transparent declared preference weights over the existing curtailment-hour proxy and predicted SoC reference. Surplus may generate an existing-owner greywater shadow proposal; a low predicted trough may generate an existing-owner night-load override OFF proposal. These are hypothetical options, with all resource effects unknown, confidence unavailable and no measured benefit claim. Baseline model confidence never qualifies causal action response. Reserve checks therefore cannot admit these options until separate qualified effects are supplied through a future qualified integration. Missing or stale battery evidence generates no optional action candidates. A typed NO_ACTION baseline is retained separately.

Thermal comfort diagnostics count hourly baseline forecast samples outside explicit configured temperature bounds over the chosen horizon. They add no ventilation calculations, airflow estimates, actuator family or qualification. The existing provisional 24-hour trajectory remains unchanged and low confidence.

The bounded 1-MiB sealed receipt retains common snapshot and registry identities, complete mathematical model inputs, prepared thermal origin/artifact/forcing/runtime bindings, declared coordination/configuration, projections, generated candidates, objective units and a sealed plan receipt. Replay recomputes models and generation before comparing the entire record. Modified derived outputs cannot survive even a resealed outer receipt.

`household_pipeline.py --receipt /private/receipt.json` reads an owned mode0600 regular file, verifies the actual household runtime and, when present, the separate strong thermal execution closure, rederives everything offline, rechecks files/runtime and emits stdout only. It refuses special files, unexpected/public inputs and changed runtime with sanitized errors. No file/SQL/live reads occur inside the pure pipeline. Source-file verification belongs to `prepare_projection` at collection; mathematical replay does not independently recertify original source archives or bank/model qualification.
