# Original measured-weather source context

The household snapshot/v6 retains the exact original radiation/v2 and rain/v1 receiver envelopes, explicit policies, stream bindings and availability clocks. Model and shadow plan receipts/v5 carry these originals through root, distinct candidate and enclosing outcome replay. Every original producer, including the collector and four weather validators, is required by the operational replay closure. Prior snapshot and receipt versions retain their original contracts.

The three point roles are received lux, lux-derived radiation proxy and the original cumulative rain counter. Decoder and receiver times are retained separately; neither is promoted to a sensor measurement timestamp. The proxy is not calibrated irradiance, and the counter is not rainfall rate or a qualified daily total. Calibration, physical effects and automatic actuation remain unqualified; planner authority is none.

The fixed collector reads the existing localhost radiation/rain endpoints and bounded owned policy files. It disables proxies and redirects and checks policies, runtime, owner epochs, cumulative counters and record consistency before and after acquisition. Identical snapshots and original expiry transitions are allowed; unchanged source counters cannot support conflicting records. All reads and final verification share one fifteen-second monotonic deadline. Each HTTP request has an additional two-second total deadline, covering headers and body even when bytes trickle. This Linux CLI uses an exclusive main-thread alarm and refuses an already active timer rather than replacing it; the prior handler is restored on every exit.

Missing, malformed, ordinary HTTP failures, timeouts, expired and independently unbound sources remain unavailable without discarding their valid companion. Arbitrary destinations, redirects, unsafe policy files, owner conflicts, policy/runtime drift and common-deadline exhaustion refuse acquisition. Radiation decoder evidence after the common model clock cannot qualify; historical decoder tolerance is unchanged. Radiation/v1 originals remain diagnostic because they lack fault visibility. Original source expiry is never renewed by reading it.
