# Household shadow coordination and decision replay

`household.planning.plan` evaluates at most eight typed intents over an explicit 6-, 12- or 24-hour horizon. It selects the maximum declared preference score by exhaustive search over at most 256 subsets. The input and output scope is `declared_shadow_bounds`; this is coordination mathematics, with no request, notification, publication or provider path.

Every candidate must pass identity/expiry checks, original battery-source freshness, registered capability, owner availability/idle state, cooldown, curfew, operator lockout, manual override and battery reserve checks. Missing facts stay unknown. The existing 10-percent emergency reserve cannot be weakened by input; stricter reserves are allowed. Combined effects must preserve the declared floor between the reserve and 100 percent. At most one proposal is selected per existing owner. Hard failures and unknowns are excluded before utility is considered. An empty optional set does not claim to repair a baseline already below reserve.

Coordination facts are closed bounded original **declarations** with their own exact raw bytes, SHA256, snapshot/owner/kind/horizon bindings, receipt clocks and at most 120-second validity. Parsing a declaration does not certify its physical truth. Source-specific live adapters and qualified model bounds remain required before operational use. The first stage accepts the explicit declaration schema for tests and offline scenarios; it never turns a bare boolean into live authority. Current input qualification and counterfactual model qualification remain distinct.

The objective unit is normalized declared preference points, bounded per candidate. It is not a claim of measured kWh, water savings or causal benefit. The selected set, component scores, hard checks and stable rejection/defer/supersession reasons are retained. No optional action and greedy scores are calculated. Actual household and current-owner policy baselines explicitly remain unavailable until their original evidence is integrated.

`seal_plan` freezes complete decision inputs and outputs, runtime identities and optional model/forecast references into a bounded 1-MiB private receipt. `replay_plan` retains each candidate’s original validation snapshot and clock, reconstructs typed inputs in that context, then revalidates them at the decision clock. Expired, newly stale and snapshot-mismatched candidates reproduce their recorded rejection. It verifies snapshot/registry identities and rederives the selected set, constraints and objective identically. Provided model/forecast references bind identities and availability; this stage does not independently recompute their domain trajectories or score later physical outcomes. Those are still full-program requirements.

`household_plan.py --receipt /private/plan.json` verifies a private owned mode0600 regular file against the actual executing source manifest, rechecks files/runtime before output and emits only stdout. Symlinks, FIFOs, oversized/invalid records and changed runtime are refused with sanitized errors. Run through `scripts/run-household-job.py` so RAM admission and CPU sharing account for concurrent development and production work; see [the shared resource policy](2026-10-14-shared-job-resources.md). All replay data stays outside Git.

A probe used three real retained native-temperature originals at their frozen as-of time. All three input roles qualified, but battery/model bounds and coordination facts were absent. The proposal therefore remained unknown and no optional action was selected, despite large declared utility. Pure and CLI decision replay were identical. This demonstrates the missing-input gate and replay, not a qualified model-backed household plan or completion of the initial milestone.

Remaining work includes unified energy/weather/action/authority inputs, source-specific coordination adapters, model-backed candidate generation, trajectory rederivation, chronological outcomes, resource drift, operator observability and per-intent release qualification. Existing owner rules remain the sole live execution gates; the translator remains permanently disabled in this program stage.
