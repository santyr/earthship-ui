# Household chronological outcome scoring

`household.outcome_origin.prepare_outcome_origin` verifies a retained original provisional forecast through the existing source/model preparation path and checks both actual persisted numeric/main delivery receipts. The immutable envelope retains original archive, exact raw receipt states and prepared projection. Its pure constructor rederives numerical output and original publication/delivery identity without rereading mutable files.

`household.outcomes.seal_outcomes` first replays the household model/candidate/plan receipt, binds its thermal output to that delivered original, then derives each later native-v2 endpoint from exact original query packets. Supported horizons are 1, 6, 12 and 24 hours within the declared planner horizon. A five-minute maturity delay is enforced. Each packet has one exact target, the same frozen assessment clock, the strict indoor source policy and the original forecast's sensor hardware epoch. Wrong/future/extra context refuses. Missing data and native null barriers stay unavailable; immature endpoints never acquire truth or a zero error.

The bounded 4-MiB score receipt retains all original inputs plus model/persistence predictions, qualified air-temperature truth, signed/absolute errors, exact source/observation identities and endpoint comfort diagnostics. Measured mass does not score latent model state. A single SoC reading does not score a predicted overnight minimum; energy outcomes remain unavailable pending qualified night coverage. Source-linked owner feasibility, deterministic-policy comparison and counterfactual uncertainty remain unavailable until their own evidence contracts are integrated.

The initial evaluation scope is explicitly `retrospective_planner_from_original_delivered_forecast`. Its original thermal forecast was actually delivered before the outcomes; the household plan was reconstructed later. It must not be presented as an as-issued production planner deployment or as measured benefit from its hypothetical actions. Causal benefit, graduation and automatic actuation stay false.

`summarize_outcomes` replays every score before aggregation. It refuses repeated scores/reassessments of an original issued endpoint and mixed model/runtime/sensor revisions. Cohorts contain at most 32 receipts and 8MiB. Reports retain raw pair count, unique source observations and conservative local issue-date units; daily units receive equal weight. `independence_assessed` remains false. Repeated rolling forecasts do not acquire independent-information status merely because they increase a counter.

`household_outcomes.py --receipt /private/score.json` requires an owned mode0600 regular file. It verifies the full original planning dependency set against actual source hashes, the complete scoring runtime and the separate strong thermal execution closure. Additional installed observational modules do not need to be falsely inserted into an older plan's manifest, but an incomplete original dependency manifest cannot pass the CLI. Replay rederives all inputs/results, rechecks files/runtime before stdout and rejects future assessments or special files with sanitized errors. No live read, action request, notification, write or service installation is exposed by the CLI.

Validation: 20 focused scoring contracts and 475 combined household/forecast tests passed under the standard strict local caps. A genuine originally delivered thermal forecast was reverified against actual persisted receipts, and three mature native endpoints were acquired through existing read-only transports under the existing consumer lock. The 1/6/12h outcomes qualified; 24h remained immature. Pure and private CLI score replay were identical. One local issue-date unit is represented, with no independence or release claim. Original telemetry and measured errors remain in private staging outside Git.

Busy shared consumers are respected. Diagnostic acquisition stops on the nonblocking lock; no producer is restarted or interrupted. The production scorer's temporary withheld result was observed separately, and later unit state showed it exited normally. This stage does not infer persistent production health from one diagnostic probe.
