# Household completed-night energy outcomes

`household.energy_outcome_sources` retains exact atomic BMS receipt strings, persistence clocks, an independently declared producer stream, and the original physical bank configuration. Packets are bounded to 4MiB and 10,000 rows; a bounded read-only collector detects overflow with a 10,001-row limit and preserves null barriers. Dedicated transactions require repeatable read, read-only status, two-second statement limits, one-second lock limits and a shared acquisition deadline. There is no numeric BMS_SOC fallback.

Pure replay invokes the existing atomic assessor for prediction-day20:00 through following-day11:00 America/Denver. At least90% validated coverage is required. Incomplete nights, missing coverage and a physical bank cutover never supply a minimum. Original bank intervals are validated for overlap, unique identities, explicit boundaries and bounded capacities. Raw malformed, unavailable and null rows terminate carry; they are retained rather than filtered out.

BMS producer stream IDs are software incarnations: the owner allocates a random UUID when its private cache resets. They are distinct from the physical bank epoch. Every packet checks its declared stream against the original rows. Scoring across a producer restart additionally requires an independently fresh fixed-owner receipt, with exact bytes/digest and availability/expiry clocks retained in the packet. An unbound, expired, future or inconsistent restart refuses.

`household.energy_outcomes` replays the original household model/plan, validates exact original Forecast_Prediction_Receipt_JSON bytes and persistence clocks, and cross-checks its original atomic SoC identity and numerical trough. The forecast must issue and persist before the target night. The measured night must match the original daily-model target and physical bank interval, and the original numerical capacity must match that bank. The normal06:40 daily issue targets the following-day11:00 minimum, beyond the optional6/12/24h planner horizon. Receipts retain both the daily target and planner horizon and explicitly state whether the outcome falls within that horizon; diagnostic scoring does not extend planner authority. Scores contain signed/absolute percentage-point errors and reserve diagnostics. Missing model, original delivery or outcome evidence stays unavailable, never zero.

The evaluation scope is retrospective planner evaluation from an originally delivered forecast. Current bank configuration establishes the measurement interval and numerical-capacity comparison; it cannot manufacture missing historical bank provenance. `original_bank_provenance_qualified`, causal benefit, graduation and automatic actuation remain false.

`household_energy_outcomes.py --receipt /private/score.json` requires an owned mode0600 regular file, full original planning dependencies, current scoring dependencies including the companion trough assessor, and the separate thermal closure when thermal inputs are present. Replay performs no live reads. The CLI rederives/rechecks before stdout, refuses future clocks and special files, and sanitizes errors.
